### 2026-09-10 — Fixed avatars rendering flush against the screen edge in list rows (a real Paper `List.Item` contract bug, not just spacing)

### 2026-09-10 — Every data-loading hook now refetches on focus, not just on mount

### 2026-09-04 — Real Android bug: date/time pickers crashed on cancel ("Cannot read property 'dismiss' of undefined")
